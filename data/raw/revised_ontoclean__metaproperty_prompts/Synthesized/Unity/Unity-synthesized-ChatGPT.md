## Unity — Carries a Unity Condition

Classify a class as **+U** when all of its instances are wholes under a single principled unity criterion: being that kind of thing determines what parts belong to one instance and where that instance stops. The criterion may be biological, physical, functional, morphological, social, institutional, or intentional. Classify a class as **~U** when every instance is necessarily a non-whole or arbitrarily demarcated amount, sum, stuff, region, or aggregate, so its boundary could be redrawn without violating the kind. Classify a class as **-U** when the class does not provide one common unity criterion, even though its instances may individually be unified in different ways. Use **~U** rather than **-U** when anti-unity holds for all instances. Unity concerns part-whole boundaries at a time, not identity over time, complexity, physical contact, or mere collectiveness.
