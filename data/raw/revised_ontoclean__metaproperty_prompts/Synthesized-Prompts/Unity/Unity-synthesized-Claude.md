# Unity (U)

A property **carries unity** when *being that kind of thing* tells you where the thing stops. For any instance and any candidate part of the world, it answers: is this part of *that* instance, or not? The answer must come from the kind itself, not from an arbitrary decision.

Three qualifications:

1. **Boundaries, not complexity.** Unity is not about how many parts a thing has or how intricate it is. A featureless marble and an elementary particle are maximally unified. Nor does it require contact — a nation's territory and a constellation are unified without being contiguous.
2. **At a time, not over time.** Unity asks "which parts?" at a moment. Persons replace their cells and remain `+U`; part-change over time is an identity question.
3. **Independent of identity.** *Amount of Matter* is `+I` and `~U` — same matter, same amount, yet no principled boundary. This is the pairing agents find hardest; never infer one metaproperty from the other.

**`+U`** — all instances are wholes under **one and the same** criterion. Name it: *topological* (*Rock*, *Island*), *morphological* (*Statue*, *Constellation*), *functional* (*Car*, *Hammer*), *social/intentional* (*Committee*, *String Quartet*, *Nation*), *biological* (*Organism*, *Tree*). Aggregates can be `+U`; the word "group" decides nothing.

**`−U`** — the property supplies no *common* criterion. Its instances may each be genuine wholes, but by unrelated criteria. This is the label for classes that cut across kinds: *Legal Agent* (persons one way, corporations another), *Red Thing*, *Physical Object*.

**`~U`** — every instance can be **arbitrarily demarcated**; the line could always have been drawn elsewhere with an equally good instance resulting: *Amount of Water*, *Amount of Matter*, *Gold*, *Sand*, *Arbitrary Spatial Region*, *Undetached Rabbit Part*.

**Test (arbitrary redrawing):** take an instance and add or remove a bit at random. Is the result still a perfectly good instance of the same kind, with no sense that anything has been damaged or left incomplete? Remove a wheel from a *Car* and you have a damaged car → not `~U`. Remove a millilitre from an *Amount of Water* and you have a perfectly good amount of water → `~U`.

Record the criterion, not just the label — `+U (functional)` rather than `+U`. A `−U` verdict should come with two named conflicting criteria. Note that `+U` is inherited downward, and a `~U` class must not subsume a `+U` class (*Statue* under *Amount of Clay* is a violation, not a labelling choice).
