Here is a simplified, AI-friendly alternative for the **Own Identity (O)** metaproperty. 

The goal here is to distinguish between a concept that *creates* the rule for tracking an object versus a concept that merely *borrows* that rule from a deeper, more fundamental category (like a role or a temporary phase).

### Proposed AI-Friendly Definition: Own Identity (O)

**Own Identity (O)** – Is this concept the **original source** of the rule we use to track and distinguish the object, or does it borrow that rule from a more fundamental category?

*   **+O (Originator of Identity)**: Yes, this concept supplies the fundamental tracking rule. It defines what the object essentially is. If you want to know how to identify this thing, this category gives you the answer. 
    *   *Examples:* Person (defines the rule for tracking a human being), Physical Object (defines the rule for tracking a physical mass in space and time).
*   **-O (Borrower / No Identity)**: No. This concept either borrows its tracking rule from a deeper "parent" category, or it doesn't have a tracking rule at all. These are often roles, phases, or descriptive traits.
    *   *Examples:* Student (borrows its identity rule from "Person"—you track a student by tracking the *person*), Caterpillar (borrows from "Lepidoptera/Insect"), Red (has no tracking rule to supply).

**IMPORTANT CONSTRAINT:** 
If a concept is **+O**, it **MUST** also be **+I** (Identifiable). A concept cannot be the original source of a tracking rule if it doesn't have a tracking rule in the first place.

To help the AI agent determine if a concept supplies its *Own* Identity, provide it with these practical tests:

**The "Parent Category" Test:**
*   Does this concept rely on a deeper, more basic noun to explain how we tell instances apart?
    *   *If YES* $\rightarrow$ **-O** (A "Teacher" relies on "Person" for its identity rules. "Teacher" just borrows it).
    *   *If NO* $\rightarrow$ likely **+O** ("Person" doesn't rely on a deeper concept to explain human identity).

**The "Role vs. Essence" Test:**
*   Is this concept just a temporary role or a job that the object is currently doing?
    *   *If YES* $\rightarrow$ **-O** (Roles like "President," "Customer," or "Pet" never supply their own identity; they borrow it from the underlying human or animal).
    *   *If NO* $\rightarrow$ potentially **+O** (Essences like "Human," "Dog," or "Car" supply the fundamental identity).
