Here is a simplified, AI-friendly alternative for the **Rigidity (R)** metaproperty. 

The goal here is to translate the philosophical concept of "possible worlds" and "essentiality" into practical questions about **permanence, survival, and temporary states**. 

### Proposed AI-Friendly Definition: Rigidity (R)

**Rigidity (R)** – Can an object lose this label or property and still continue to exist as the same fundamental thing?

*   **+R (Rigid / Permanent Essence)**: No. This label is a permanent, essential fact about the object. If the object stops being this, it ceases to exist entirely (it is dead or destroyed). It cannot "take a break" from being this.
    *   *Examples:* Person, Dog, Physical Object. *(A human cannot stop being a "Person" without dying; a dog cannot temporarily become a non-dog).*
*   **-R (Non-Rigid / Mixed Permanence)**: Sometimes. This label is not strictly permanent for *everything*. Some objects might have this property permanently, but other objects can gain or lose it and still survive. 
    *   *Examples:* Red Thing, Hard Object. *(A ruby is permanently red, but a car can be painted blue and still be the same car. Therefore, "Red Thing" is non-rigid).*
*   **~R (Anti-Rigid / Always Temporary)**: Yes, always. For *every single instance* of this concept, the label is temporary or conditional. The object can (or eventually will) lose this label and still continue to exist as the same underlying entity. These are usually roles, jobs, or phases of life.
    *   *Examples:* Student, Employee, Child, Caterpillar. *(Every student can graduate and stop being a student, but they still exist as a person. Every child becomes an adult).*

To help the AI agent determine the Rigidity of a concept, provide it with these practical thought experiments:

**The "Cease to Exist" Test:**
*   If an instance of this concept suddenly stops being [Concept], is it completely destroyed or dead?
    *   *If YES for all instances* $\rightarrow$ **+R** (If a Tree stops being a Tree, it's just dead wood—it doesn't exist as a tree anymore).
    *   *If NO for all instances* $\rightarrow$ **~R** (If a Mayor stops being a Mayor, they are not destroyed, they are just a regular citizen again).

**The "Role vs. Species" Test:**
*   Is this concept a temporary job, a societal role, or a phase of life that something *goes through*?
    *   *If YES* $\rightarrow$ likely **~R** (Puppy, Customer, Patient).
    *   *If NO, it's what the thing fundamentally IS* $\rightarrow$ likely **+R** (Animal, Human, Mountain).

**The "Paint/Change" Test (for -R):**
*   Is this concept a physical trait (like a color, shape, or size) where some things have it by nature (can't lose it) but other things just have it by accident (can lose it)?
    *   *If YES* $\rightarrow$ **-R** ("Red" is -R because a ruby must be red, but a painted wall can change color).
